Website Maintenance

Keep Your Website Updated and Working

SixTech Solutions provides website maintenance for small businesses, personal ministries, organizations, and individuals. We help keep websites updated, organized, and working properly so our clients can focus on their work.

Website Support When You Need It

A website needs regular attention even after it has been designed and launched. Content may need to be updated, links can stop working, information can become outdated, and small technical problems can affect the experience of visitors.

SixTech Solutions can help with common website maintenance tasks such as updating text and images, checking links, making small design changes, reviewing pages, and helping troubleshoot website problems.

Maintenance That Fits Your Needs

Some websites need ongoing support while others only need help once in a while. Our goal is to provide a simple maintenance option based on the type of help each website needs.

Maintenance Options

Choose the Type of Support You Need

Website owners have different maintenance needs, so SixTech Solutions offers both one-time help and ongoing monthly maintenance.

One-Time Maintenance

This option is for website owners who need help with a specific update or problem. The website can be reviewed first so we can understand the work that needs to be completed.

Examples may include updating website content, replacing images, fixing broken links, making small layout changes, or troubleshooting a website issue.

Monthly Maintenance

This option is designed for website owners who need ongoing help keeping their website updated. Regular maintenance can help make sure important information stays current and the website continues working properly.
